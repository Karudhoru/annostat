# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Annostat contributors, maintainers, and community members are committed to a
welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment. Everyone should be able
to participate regardless of identity, background, experience, ability, or
personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Community members are expected to:

- communicate with empathy, patience, and professional respect;
- welcome constructive disagreement and evidence-based scientific discussion;
- give and receive feedback without personal attacks;
- acknowledge mistakes, correct them, and credit the work of others; and
- protect private or sensitive information shared in confidence.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or sexualized
language, intimidation, trolling, deliberate disruption, threats, publishing
private information without permission, and conduct that would be inappropriate
in a professional research environment.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
project events, and public representation of Annostat. Maintainers may edit or
remove content, reject contributions, restrict participation, or apply a
temporary or permanent ban when behavior violates this policy. Enforcement
decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and protect the safety
and privacy of reporters.

Report an incident privately to the project maintainer using the contact method
listed on the [repository owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Karudhoru).
Do not disclose sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Reports will be
reviewed promptly, fairly, and with appropriate confidentiality.
